ABOUT MEDICARE

Careful service, connected work.

MediCare combines a welcoming customer experience with focused, secure tools for every pharmacy team member.

OUR APPROACH

Every request has a responsible next step.

Customers can browse available medicine records, upload prescriptions, and send feedback. Inside the pharmacy, role-based workspaces make it clear who verifies, prepares, sells, promotes, procures, and follows up each part of the journey.

The system is designed as a connected pharmacy workflow backed by SQL Server, Spring Boot, Java, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
